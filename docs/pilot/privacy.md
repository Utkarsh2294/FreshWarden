# Privacy and retention preparation

Engineering controls are not a claim of legal compliance. Have the operator's reviewer confirm applicable commencement dates, rules, notices, processing basis, processor contracts, grievance channel and retention policy before a real pilot. [MeitY's official DPDP Act 2023 text](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023-1.pdf) addresses notices, protection duties and individuals' rights; actual obligations depend on the processing and applicable provisions.

Collect only the account name, role, phone identifier and store assignments needed for access. Do not photograph faces, customer/order addresses or payment information, or monitor employee performance. Evidence includes product/shelf photos, attribution, timestamps and hashes. Prompts ignore people, but automatic face blurring is **not implemented**: retake such images before upload.

Configured providers receive the selected image or supplied report facts. Use staged data with free prototype tiers; verify current training/retention/location/commercial terms before a pilot. Hashes are not anonymization or proof a photographed condition was true. Keys and photos are excluded from request logs.

Readings have a 30-day asynchronous TTL. Evidence, reports, incidents, snapshots and accounts currently have no automatic retention purge. Define and test retention/deletion/backup procedures before rollout. Local queued photos/drafts remain until sync or explicit removal; use separate OS/browser profiles on shared devices. Browser storage is not application-level encrypted.

Inspector tokens are hashed, expiring and revocable; the original URL is a credential and must not be posted publicly. It exposes only one store's read-only evidence. Access counters remain in the database. Metrics require an admin account. Restrict logs and backups to designated operators.

For an incident, stop external sharing, revoke affected credentials, preserve restricted audit evidence, identify affected data/recipients and follow the operator's reviewed response process. External notification is a human operator action under that process.
